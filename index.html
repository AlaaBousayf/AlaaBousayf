About

I'm a software engineer who fell for design. I care about the moment a screen just makes sense, in Arabic and in English.

My graduation research measured the usability of banking software in Libya: 135 bank users, the ISO 9241-10 framework and a comparison between an international system and the local "Almezan" system. It taught me to back design decisions with evidence.

I placed first in Future Technology's TGP graduate program and now work there as a software engineer, supporting a partner bank, helping launch its mobile banking app and redesigning the company website.
